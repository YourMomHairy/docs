Spouse History is a tool that helps users view the history of their marriages and understand the relationship between spouses easily. It offers users insight into their current and previous relationship history making it easy to understand their relationship records.

Spouse History Viewer can serve various beneficial purposes for users such as:

- Users can reflect on their past relationships, identifying patterns or trends that may influence future partner choices.
- Provides a good indication of whether their past relationship involves someone you knew, allowing users to act accordingly.
- Promoting transparency and understanding in their relationships.
- Users can analyze their relationship histories to identify what traits or interest they seek in a partner.
