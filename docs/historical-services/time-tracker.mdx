The Time Tracker is a tool designed to help users monitor the time spent interacting with various users and rooms, providing them with an idea of how actively they are engaging within those rooms.

This tool is often used by:
<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="Chill Space" defaultOpen="true" icon="people-roof">
Users can identify their favorite rooms and hotspots for social interaction.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Favouritism" defaultOpen="true" icon="star">
Analyzing the times of day when users spend the most time with specific individuals or in certain rooms.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Hiding Identity" defaultOpen="true" icon="eye-slash">
Verifying whether someone is genuinely offline or spending time with another user instead.
</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
