  <Tab title="Keyframes" icon="timeline">
    This view highlights the keyframe timeline (red box). You can add or remove keyframes, then play or step through the timeline to see how the avatar animates. It helps you preview timing and detailed motion without writing code.

    Source: [What is keyframes?](https://www.adobe.com/creativecloud/video/discover/keyframing.html)

  <Tab title="Avatar Position" icon="arrow-down-arrow-up">
  Allows you to move the avatar in any direction including upside down — for full control over its orientation and posing.
  - <span style={{ color: "#f63b3b" }}>X</span> (left/right) moves the avatar left or right.
  - <span style={{ color: "#41f63b" }}>Y</span> (forward/back) moves the avatar closer or farther away.
  - <span style={{ color: "#3b67f6" }}>Z</span> (up/down) moves the avatar up or down.
<br />
    <Frame caption="Overview">
      ![](/images/premium-tools/pose-maker/move-avatar.png)
    </Frame>
  </Tab>
