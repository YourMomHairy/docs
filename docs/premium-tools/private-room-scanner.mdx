This service is designed to be straightforward for our premium users, It provides a list of participates including their outfits wore in that specific room.

- **Participants**: Reveal list of users who are in the private room.
- **Outfits**: Display outfits worn in the specific room and also "Try On" any products.
- **Furniture**: Shows you list of furnitures displayed in a private room.

There is alot of confusion when it comes to searching a user but we will do our best to break it down into simple terms for you to understand.

If you search for **Alwin** and you believe or curious if any of **Alwin**’s private rooms are currently open. If the results are shown discovered you are in luck, this means one of Alwin’s private room is active.
However keep in mind results only appear for rooms created by the __searched user__. If they join someone else's room, it won't be detected.

<Badge stroke color="orange">GaleDevon</Badge> joins <Badge stroke color="blue">Alwin</Badge> private room and you scan <Badge stroke color="orange">GaleDevon</Badge>, you won’t find any results. Instead you need to search <Badge stroke color="blue">Alwin</Badge> who is the private room owner not <Badge stroke color="orange">GaleDevon</Badge>.

### I don’t want VuArchives scanning my rooms, can I hide my private rooms?

We do not offer the option to hide or block your private room or outfits on VuArchives.

The information collected by our system is publicly accessible and similar data is readily available on other platforms as well. Even if we were to introduce such a feature in the future, users would still be able to access this information through alternative services.

We encourage you to save your money and avoid unnecessary gimmicks that promise to “hide” or “block” this information on other websites or platforms, as there will always be other services that can display this information.
